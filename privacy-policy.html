Privacy Policy

Vaishno Pearl Flat Owners Maintenance Mutually Aided Co-operative Society Ltd (FOMACS)
Kompally, Hyderabad, Telangana, India · Last updated: 21 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Vaishno Pearl FOMACS app ("the app") handles information. The app is used by the society's office-bearers and flat owners to manage maintenance collection, payments, receipts, documents and resident communication.

1. Where your data is stored

The app stores all data locally on your own device, inside the app's storage on that device. The app does not send your data to any server owned by the society or the developer, and there is no central database or cloud account. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, the data on that device is removed.

2. What information the app holds

Depending on how the society uses it, the app may hold: flat numbers and owner/tenant names, contact phone numbers, monthly maintenance and payment records, expense and vendor-payment entries, digital signatures drawn by office-bearers, uploaded documents and notices, and meeting/poll details. This information is entered by the society's office-bearers or owners themselves.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to operate the society's maintenance and administration features on the device — for example, to calculate dues, generate receipts and vouchers, send reminders, and keep records. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell or share your data with third parties. When you choose to send a reminder, receipt or message, the app opens your own WhatsApp, SMS or phone app, and you decide whether to send. Any sharing then happens through those apps under their own terms.

5. Permissions

The app may ask to open WhatsApp/SMS/phone for sending messages or calling, and to access storage for saving or attaching documents and receipts. These are used only for the actions you start.

6. Children

The app is an administrative tool for a housing society and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same link.
